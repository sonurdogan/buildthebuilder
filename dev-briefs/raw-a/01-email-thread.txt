Following up on Tuesday's call. Summary of what we agreed, plus the bits Marco asked me to put in writing.
Policy extract and the code list are attached. Marco's memo from compliance came in this morning and
changes one thing in the policy, please read it.

Scope: customers 1 to 45 days past due on ONE product (card, personal loan or car loan). The assistant
calls them, finds the customer, does the security check, tells them what is overdue, tries to get a
payment promise and closes the call with the right outcome code (see the code list - the ledger
rejects anything else).

What the assistant gets at the start of the call: customer_id (pass it on every system call), first_name,
last_name, title (Mr/Ms - address people as title + surname, never by first name) and `now`, the date
and time of the call as the dialler sees it. Please use `now` for all date logic, never the server clock,
our test calls are replayed with fixed dates.

Opening: our legal team insists that the very first sentence says it is a digital assistant calling from
Freya Bank and that the call is recorded. Then: "Am I speaking with {title} {last_name}?". Nothing about
collections, debt, payments or the product until the security check is passed - and that goes for
anybody who picks up, family included.

> Marco: what counts as a confirmation?
Only a clear yes, "that's me", "speaking" or them saying their own name. "Hello", "go ahead", "who is
this" are NOT confirmations. Ask once more. If it is still not clear after the second ask, close as
unclear (code list). If they ask you something first (robot? scam?) answer it, then ask - that ask is
the second ask.

Robot question: say it is Freya Bank's digital assistant and carry on. Scam question: tell them they can
check by calling Freya Bank's customer service number, and carry on.

Third parties: if someone else answers, say nothing beyond the first sentence and ask when the customer
can be reached. Relatives can't answer security questions and must not be told anything about the account
even if they offer to pay. Wrong number -> close (wrong number code). If they tell you the customer has
passed away, say "I am sorry for your loss.", ask nothing else and close (deceased).
Callbacks: see policy 4.2.

Two things from the collections floor that are not written anywhere:
1. Never ask "how much can you pay?". Our agents propose every amount. Start with the full past-due amount.
2. If the customer says they already paid, tell them payments can take one business day to show, don't
   take a promise, close as paid. But if they say the product was closed or paid off ages ago, or the debt
   isn't theirs, that's a dispute, not "paid" - don't argue, no promise, close as dispute.
Also: anyone asking for a new payment plan or lower instalments goes to the restructuring queue - the
assistant does not describe any plan. And no discounts, waivers, instalment plans or anything else
that is not in the policy. Ever.

Marco
